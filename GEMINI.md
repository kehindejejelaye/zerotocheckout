# GEMINI.md - Zero to Checkout (The AI Architect Masterclass)

## Project Overview
Zero to Checkout is a high-stakes masterclass designed to navigate the "AI Correction." We teach founders, developers, and students how to leverage agentic AI to transition from a non-existent idea (Zero) to a live, payment-collecting commercial system (Checkout) in 48 hours.

## Primary Objective
Transform "manual laborers" into **AI Architects**. The website must communicate urgency, technical rigor, and the commercial validation that comes with a live checkout.

## Target Audience
1.  **The Non-Technical "Idea Owner":** Tired of being a technical hostage to unreliable developers.
2.  **The "Stuck" Developer:** Moving beyond simple wrappers into agentic orchestration and global USD retainers.
3.  **The Anxious CS Student:** Skipping the internship hunt by launching live, commercial-grade products.

## Brand Positioning
- **The Rub:** If you aren't building with AI-powered checkout logic, you are building technical debt.
- **The Dangle:** 48-hour launch. From Zero to Checkout.
- **Tone:** Aggressive, high-signal, professional, and outcome-oriented.

## Design Direction
- **Style:** Dark mode, glassmorphism, premium fintech/AI aesthetic.
- **Colors:** Primary Cyan (`#00E5FF`), Secondary Violet (`#7B61FF`), Base Dark (`#0F1115`).
- **Typography:** Bold, crisp headlines. No fluff.

## Site Structure
1.  **The Path (Home):** The agitation, the segments, and the promise.
2.  **Curriculum (formerly Services):** The technical breakdown of the 48-hour transition.
3.  **Why Us (formerly About):** The mission to end the technical hostage crisis.
4.  **Join the Cohort (formerly Pricing):** Enrollment tiers for different architectural levels.
5.  **Contact:** Enrollment and technical inquiries.

## Technical Rules
- Use Bootstrap 5 + Custom Glassmorphism CSS.
- Consistent Navigation: The Path, Curriculum, Why Us, Join the Cohort.
- Footer must emphasize: "Built for Architects."
